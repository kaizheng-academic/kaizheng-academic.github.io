TRACE Overview

Restoring the RNA-Ligand Interaction Manifold via Topology-Preserving Contrastive Learning under Epistemic Uncertainty.

High-throughput RNA-ligand screening is fundamentally affected by Unknown-as-Negative (UaN) bias, where unlabeled positives are treated as negatives. We show that naive ERM under UaN introduces decoy gradients and causes a topological rupture of the structure-activity manifold. TRACE addresses this failure mode with a theoretically grounded learning framework that restores manifold continuity and improves reliability.

Main Contributions

  • Risk rectification: confidence-weighted importance sampling with uniform convergence under posterior drift.
  • Optimization stability: thermodynamic temperature regulation that bounds Jacobian factors and enforces local Lipschitz continuity.
  • Discriminative alignment: margin-aware contrastive regularization with exponential tail guarantees for misordering risk.
  • Empirical gains: 18.2% relative AUPRC improvement, 1.73× higher resilience under 50% noise, and over 83% ECE reduction.
